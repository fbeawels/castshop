# Canada Post Logo

## Summary
The image is a logo for Canada Post, the country's primary postal service. It features a clean, modern design with a prominent use of red and white, the national colors of Canada. The logo effectively communicates the brand's identity and purpose.

- The elements are symmetrically arranged, creating a balanced and harmonious composition.
