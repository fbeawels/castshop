# Credit Card Security Code Visualization

## Summary
This image displays two credit cards illustrating the location of the Card Verification Value (CVV) code for different card networks. The purpose is to educate users on where to find the security code on their credit cards.

## Detailed Description

### Overall Layout and Structure
The image is divided into two sections, each showing a different type of credit card:
1. The top section shows a Visa, Mastercard, or Discover card.
2. The bottom section shows an American Express card.

### Main Components and Arrangement

#### Top Card (Visa, Mastercard, Discover)
- **Card Body**: Gold color with a brown strip at the top.
- **Card Network Logos**: Visa, Mastercard, and Discover logos are displayed on the top right corner.
- **Cardholder Name**: "MR. JOHN SMITH" is printed on the top left.
- **Card Number**: Partially visible as "4123 6789 1234 5678".
- **Expiration Date**: "01/05" is displayed below the card number.
- **CVV Code**: "987" is prominently displayed in a red circle on the right side of the card.

#### Bottom Card (American Express)
- **Card Body**: Dark blue color.
- **Card Network Logo**: American Express logo is displayed at the top.
- **Cardholder Name**: "MR. JOHN SMITH" is printed on the bottom.
- **Card Number**: Partially visible as "3723 678901 23456".
- **Expiration Date**: "01/05" is displayed on the right side.
- **CVV Code**: "9876" is prominently displayed in a red circle on the right side of the card.

### Visual Elements
- **Colors**:
  - Top Card: Gold body with brown strip and black text.
  - Bottom Card: Dark blue body with white text.
- **Styles**:
  - Both cards have a clean, modern design with clear, legible text.
  - The CVV codes are highlighted with red circles for easy identification.
- **Icons**:
  - Card network logos are clearly displayed.

### Text Content and Formatting
- **Cardholder Name**: Printed in uppercase letters.
- **Card Number**: Partially obscured for security reasons.
- **Expiration Date**: Formatted as "MM/YY".
- **CVV Code**: Displayed in bold within a red circle.

### Distinctive Features or Patterns
- The CVV code is located on the back of Visa, Mastercard, and Discover cards but on the front of American Express cards.
- The red circles around the CVV codes draw attention to the security feature.
- The layout and design are consistent with standard credit card formats, making it easy for users to identify and locate the CVV code on their own cards.
