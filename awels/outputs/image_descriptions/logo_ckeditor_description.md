# CKEditor Logo

## Summary
The image is a logo for CKEditor, a popular web text editor. The logo features a clean, modern design with a combination of geometric shapes and typography, conveying a sense of professionalism and user-friendliness.

The logo is composed of two main parts: an icon on the left and the text "CKEditor" on the right. The overall layout is horizontal, with the icon and text aligned side by side.
